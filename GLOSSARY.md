# Pilot

Pilot coordinates agent work around outcomes, with shared language for planning, learning and human involvement.
This glossary records agreed vocabulary, not a claim that every existing screen or document uses it yet.

## Language

### Work and uncertainty

**Mission**:
A coordinated outcome.

**Task**:
A defined piece of implementation work, distinct from exploration that reduces uncertainty.

**Exploration**:
Work that reduces uncertainty through research, a prototype or an interview, rather than implementing an agreed solution.

**Research**:
An investigation that gathers and examines evidence to answer a question.
Its findings are not user decisions or automatically accepted guidance.

**Prototype**:
A small, disposable experiment that answers a question and may produce an artifact.
Successful prototype checks are evidence, not production acceptance.

**Grilling**:
A method of challenging and developing questions, distinct from the interview document presented to the user.

**Interview**:
A saved, user-facing document of questions the user can answer.

**Fog of war**:
Relevant areas that are not yet understood well enough to define specific questions or work.

**Frontier**:
Specific questions or work whose prerequisites have been resolved.

### Agreement and human involvement

**Brief**:
The initial goal and context for a mission, distinct from its agreed spec.

**Spec**:
The agreed scope, behavior and acceptance criteria for a mission.

**Decision**:
A choice that guides work, distinct from permission for a specific action.

**Approval**:
Permission for a specific proposed action.

**Human action**:
Something the user must do outside Pilot, distinct from making a decision or giving approval.

### Understanding and outputs

**Knowledge**:
Saved project understanding with sources.

**Report**:
An account of progress, findings or results, not a user decision or automatically accepted guidance.

**Insights**:
Retrospective analysis that proposes improvements, not user decisions or automatically accepted guidance.

**Evidence**:
Observations that support a claim, distinct from acceptance of that claim or its outcome.

**Acceptance**:
An explicit determination that agreed criteria are met.

**Artifact**:
A saved output, such as a diagram or interactive preview.
An artifact may come from a prototype, but not every artifact is a prototype.

### Work controls and organization

**Pause**:
A temporary hold on mission work for selective resume.

**Stop**:
Cancellation of current execution, not an undo of completed effects.

**Archive**:
Removal of inactive work from everyday views, not a command to pause or stop work.
