Pilot, overview first

Three complete directions where the coordinator does the work and you see outcomes. None has a sidebar: navigation sits on top or in context. You never manage the coordinator's workers. Chat opens from the work it is about.

Each prototype is mock data in your browser. Buttons only change local state and say so. Use the panel in the lower right to switch between a quiet day, work underway, a decision and trouble.

D · Day Your pick

B's daily view, with what you asked for:

  • Missions: Moving / Not moving. Each row says why it stopped and links to the decision.
  • Standup notes. Yesterday, Today, Blocked. One click to copy. Yesterday and This week too.
  • New, as an interview. Small asks start right away. Bigger ones get 5 short questions, each with a recommended answer. The answers become the mission's agreement.
  • No chats to manage. No chat list. You ask or redirect from inside a mission.
  • No spending limit by default. It is an optional setting at the end of the interview.
  • Start and end the day. Morning: overnight, decide, today's plan. Evening: done, still open (kept for tomorrow), tonight, close.
  • Decide all with the keyboard. Press D. Then 1–3 to choose, Enter to confirm, L for later, ? to ask.
  • Help me decide. On every decision: Say it simpler, More context, Show me (a visual Pilot makes for that decision) and Ask a question, answered inside the card. Keys S, M, V, ? in triage. needs contract
  • Review. A review arrives as a decision. Open review shows files, the diff, Pilot's own review note and line comments, then Approve or Request changes.
  • Changes. More → Changes lists everything: waiting for you, landed by Pilot, in progress (live) and reviewed by you. Reopen any change, flag a problem, undo (asks first) or save it to look at again.
  • Automations. Next runs show in Coming up. More → Automations lists yours and Pilot's, with on/off. Type "every Monday…" in New to make one.
  • Pilot can set schedules for its own mission, like a nightly benchmark. You can skip or remove them. needs contract

Recommendation

Start from C, the Board, and borrow two things

  • It answers "what needs me" and "what is Pilot doing" in one look, without a sidebar or chats.
  • Focus mode gives the one-thing-at-a-time path that helps with ADHD, and ends with "You can close Pilot".
  • Borrow from B: the "Since you looked" sentence above the board.
  • Borrow from A: the agreed-scope panel and the stage route on the sheet.

Your choice decides the next step. Nothing is built until you pick one.

What was checked

  • Each prototype in the four states, light and dark, at 1440, 1100, 760 and 390 px wide.
  • Keyboard: Tab order, ⌘K palette, Escape closes drawers and sheets, arrow keys in menus, tabs and board columns.
  • Decide, Check again, Approve, Skip and Send change only local mock state.
  • Checked against the impeccable craft floor: no colored side stripes, no labels above headings, one primary action per view.
  • Not proven: motion feel, IME, screen readers, performance with real data, and native feasibility. The system font stands in for Inter, because the prototypes load no font files.
