Other ideas for C

The board felt generic. Here are three different ways to see the same work. Each is a quick sketch, not a full prototype. Same synthetic morning as the Decision scenario, 11:02.

1 · Now line

Your day as a timeline. Left of the line is done, right is planned. Moving work is a solid line; work waiting for you turns into a dashed line that grows until you act.

Good for

Seeing what is moving at a glance. Standups: read left to right.

Risk

Dense with many missions. Needs a list view on narrow windows.

Native

Custom canvas drawing. Times come from mission activity that exists today.

Every mission gives its standup in one row: what it finished, what it is doing, what it needs from you. The bottom row is your own standup, ready to copy.

3 · Agenda with decision windows

Pilot saves non-urgent decisions for two short windows a day that you choose. The rest of your day stays free. Only failures that stop everything can break in.

Good for

Focus. You know when Pilot will need you, and for how long.

Risk

A mission can wait hours for a decision. Urgent cases need a clear rule.

Native

Holding notifications needs a new rule. needs contract

What I would do

Keep D (Day) as the home. Add the Now line as the top of Today, so you see movement before you read anything.

Decision windows work in any concept as a setting, so I would offer them separately. The standup grid is mostly covered by D's Standup notes.
